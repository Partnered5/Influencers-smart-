# Influencer Smart — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Studio Paper
Very light editorial tooling with warm paper tones, cobalt controls, and a magazine-like canvas that makes AI creation feel intentional and human-led.
**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Signal Noir
A dark production suite with high-contrast lime signals, cinematic previews, and a command-center feel for advanced creator workflows.
**Probability:** 0.03

### Theme Name: Soft Portal
A calm, airy creator workspace using soft gradients, pale lilac surfaces, and friendly rounded modules for first-time AI creators.
**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen approach: Studio Paper

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial software: the precision of a fashion lookbook, the utility of a creative production tool, and the tactile warmth of printed studio notes.

### Core Principles
1. **Editorial confidence:** Oversized headlines, crisp metadata, and deliberate hierarchy make the product feel curated rather than generic.
2. **Warm utility:** Ivory paper, ink navy, and one ownable cobalt accent keep the interface approachable while still feeling premium.
3. **Visible authorship:** The user stays in the director’s seat. AI suggestions are framed as drafts, not autonomous truth.
4. **Structured freedom:** The UI makes it easy to move from persona to look to campaign without hiding the underlying controls.

### Color Philosophy
Use warm ivory as the working surface and ink navy as the grounding color, evoking a studio desk and printed contact sheets. Cobalt is reserved for moments of agency—generate, publish, approve—so it feels like a decisive tool rather than decoration. A small coral signal marks attention and review states.

### Layout Paradigm
Use a persistent left rail for the studio’s sections, a broad asymmetric workspace, and stacked editorial panels rather than centered marketing cards. The home canvas should feel like a dashboard spread: a live preview anchors the left, while controls and recent work create a visual rhythm to the right.

### Signature Elements
- A cobalt “spark” mark used as the brand symbol and as a recurring generative indicator.
- Contact-sheet style previews with small handwritten-feeling labels and status stamps.
- Thin ruled dividers and paper-texture surfaces to create a tactile, editorial workspace.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like making a creative decision: selection states are clear, active controls feel pressed into the page, and generated results arrive with a short “developing” state. Never imply the model has acted independently; keep the user’s prompt, approval, and disclosure choices visible.

### Animation
Use quick, physical transitions under 240ms. Panels rise by a few pixels and fade in; preview thumbnails slide into a contact sheet with a stagger; the cobalt spark rotates once during generation. Respect reduced motion and avoid looping decoration.

### Typography System
Use **DM Sans** for interface copy and controls, paired with **Bodoni Moda** for display headlines and campaign names. Headings should use tight tracking and a mix of regular and italic styles; body copy stays compact and highly legible.

### Brand Essence
**Influencer Smart is a director’s desk for building disclosed virtual creators and campaign content—fast, consistent, and made for people who care how the work lands.**
Personality: **editorial, capable, considered**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are confident and specific. CTAs are action-oriented but never hype-heavy. Microcopy explains what the user controls and how AI is being used.

Example lines:
- “Make a creator people can remember.”
- “Your brief is the source of truth.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a four-point cobalt spark nested inside a square crop bracket, suggesting both a camera frame and a moment of generation. The wordmark uses a custom high-contrast serif treatment for “Influencer” with a compact sans “SMART” stamp beneath it.

### Signature Brand Color
**Studio Cobalt — #3155D8.** It is decisive, legible, and ownable against warm ivory without leaning into the usual purple AI palette.
